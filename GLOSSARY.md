# Portfolio home screen

A personal portfolio presented as a macOS-style desktop on wide screens and an iOS/iPadOS-style home screen on touch screens.

## Widgets

**Widget**:
A small live tile on the home screen (clock, photo carousel, calendar, notes, music, coffee) that previews or opens an app.
_Avoid_: Card, tile, panel

**Widget catalogue**:
The single list of every widget and what defines it: its look and its default place and height on the desktop.
_Avoid_: Widget registry, widget specs

**Desktop board**:
The desktop arrangement of widgets on the 12-column grid, including where each one sits and the rules for dragging a widget onto free space.
_Avoid_: Widget grid, widget layout

**Height rule**:
How tall a widget is on the desktop: either a fixed height, or a ratio of its width.
_Avoid_: Auto height, widget size

## Apps

**App**:
One of the portfolio's programs (Notes, Finder, Photos, Contact, Music, VS Code) that opens in a window or sheet.
_Avoid_: Window (the frame an app is shown in), program

**App catalogue**:
The single list of every app and what defines it: its title, icon, place in the dock, and the shape of its address.
_Avoid_: App registry, APPS

**App view**:
What an app is currently showing, such as a note, or a project folder and one of its files. The address bar reflects the focused app's view.
_Avoid_: Route, state, album
