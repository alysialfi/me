## Description

This project focuses on transforming DomaiNesia's digital presence through more intuitive and customer-centric interfaces. As part of the developer (dev) team, I was in charge of developing web pages across multiple breakpoints, including in-page purchasing systems, dynamic pricing schemas, and customized promotional offers aligned with marketing requirements. The project prioritizes both user-friendliness and functionality to create a strong brand experience.

## Impacts

- Created & revamped >90% of existing landing pages
- Reduced waiting-time by creating highly efficient functions
- Improved Core Web Vitals metrics significantly, including First Contentful Paint (FCP) and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS)

## Challenge

During my second year at DomaiNesia, the dev team decided to switch tech stack from imperative to functional programming, jQuery to Vue.js. This final decision came after thorough evaluation of our existing technology ecosystem, SEO, and business workflows. The most challenging part was to map the functionality of each block of code, so discussions with the team leader and fellow colleagues were part of the daily task. This process helped me gain deeper insight into the codebase and led me to improve its efficiency by eliminating redundant parts.

## Lessons Learned

A well-performing landing page is more than just aesthetics. It must communicate the brand's identity clearly, follow a logical and user-centered flow, use concise and persuasive copy, and accommodate both new and returning visitors. Collaboration within and across teams also plays a critical role in delivering a strong and shared understanding to create a better output.
