## Description

As a student with industry experience, it's always fun to re-learn everything from the basics. Completing assignments, quizzes, forums, and exams is a mandatory part of the learning routine. During six semesters here, some of these subjects are my favorites: Data Structure, Human & Computer Interaction, Software Engineering, and Natural Language Processing.

## Impact

- Gained a deeper understanding of database indexing, leading to a 90% reduction in query execution time
- Developed more user-friendly web interfaces by focusing on design approaches suited for non-developers
- Learned how Waterfall and Agile methodologies are used in project management
- Built a solid conceptual grasp of how AI processes prompts and delivers relevant results

## Challenges

Balancing full-time work with academic responsibilities was a significant challenge. Final exams, in particular, often required intensive effort: from reading research papers to designing user interfaces, coding solutions, and constructing complex tree structures. Managing time effectively and maintaining focus were essential.

## Lessons Learned

Returning to the basics helped me realize that theoretical knowledge strengthens practical problem-solving. I learned the importance of solid fundamentals: understanding how and why things work behind the scenes makes it easier to build and debug systems in real-world projects. Balancing full-time work and studies taught me discipline, time management, and resilience. I also discovered that staying curious and willing to explore different perspectives is what keeps both my academic and professional journey exciting and meaningful.
