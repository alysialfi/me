## Description

TMLA is an IT solutions boutique based in Italy that builds custom digital products for small and medium-sized businesses, with a focus on simple, sustainable and maintainable software. As a solo freelancer working for an international client, I designed the landing page in Figma and built it end to end: hero, services, tech stacks, why-choose-us values, a note from the CEO, and a contact form.

## Impacts

- Delivered a complete landing page for an international client, from Figma design to production
- Built an EN/ID language toggle so the same page serves both audiences
- Added a contact form with privacy consent and a "Book A Call" option to turn visitors into leads
- Kept the build lightweight with plain HTML, TailwindCSS and JavaScript, matching the client's "no over-engineering" philosophy

## Challenge

TMLA's identity is built on restraint: simplicity, sustainability and quality over flashy architecture. The hard part was translating those values into a visual language that still feels confident and modern, without falling back on generic tech-agency tropes. Every section had to earn its place, and copy, spacing and visuals were iterated until the page itself reflected the way TMLA builds software.

## Lessons Learned

A landing page is the first proof of a company's values, so the design has to practice what the brand preaches. Working directly with a foreign client also taught me to communicate decisions clearly in writing, ask the right questions early, and own the whole process, from design through delivery.
