## Description

Since the end of 2022, I've had the opportunity to mentor in an online course, Project Based Internship by Rakamin Academy. This experience later led me to join the *Magang dan Studi Independen Bersertifikat (MSIB)* as a Mentor Assistant in Full-Stack Web Development. Since then, I've been invited to speak at various tech events, both internally at DomaiNesia, and externally through tech communities such as Google Developer Group (GDG) Yogyakarta, Python Jogja, and PyCon APAC 2024.

## Impact

- 99% of mentees reported satisfaction with my learning approach
- Successfully led my MSIB group to become the best-performing group in the cohort, following one member as the best student
- Established PyLadies Yogyakarta, a women-in-tech community, and organized multiple tech events

## Challenges

Preparing for events required me to be physically, mentally, and materially prepared. For a 30 to 60-minute offline workshop, I often spent up to two weeks crafting a solid pitch deck and preparing ready-to-deploy source code. Another challenge was adapting to different audience backgrounds. For instance, when I spoke in front of the *Persatuan Guru Bahasa Jawa* (Javanese Language Teachers Association) about building websites using WordPress, I had to put in an engaging hook and deliver a clear, step-by-step tutorial. This demanded extra energy and creativity to keep the session relatable for non-technical participants.

## Lessons Learned

As a mentor, I've come to understand that every person learns differently, and finding the most inclusive and engaging way to deliver material is key. I've also learned that small gestures, like giving a little gift as a reward, can keep participants motivated and excited to learn until the session ends.
