## Description

Lunash is a startup in the financial services sector, aiming to help businesses and financial institutions recover payments in a more efficient and professional manner. The platform combines AI-driven technology, strategic engagement, and human expertise. I contributed to building a dynamic dashboard that integrates both in-house and third-party APIs, providing a seamless user interface and managing financial activities.

## Impacts

- Developed 70% of the dashboard's core features
- Automated the deployment process, reducing manual effort and improving efficiency
- Optimized infrastructure by leveraging free tools that offered the same functionality as paid solutions, cutting down monthly operational costs

## Challenge

I was provided with packs of templates on day zero, asked to choose the most suitable template, and given limited time to learn a lot of documentation. Many tools and modules used in the project were new to me, which initially made it challenging to adapt. After a few weeks working in, I began to focus on automating the deployment pipeline using Google Cloud Platform (GCP), and about a year later, we migrated the entire infrastructure to Amazon Web Services (AWS). Although AWS was new to me as well, my experience with GCP helped me recognize similar patterns, making the transition smoother.

## Lessons Learned

Through this project, I learned that ReactJS has a fundamentally different approach to state management compared to VueJS, and discovered how TypeScript can greatly enhance the developer experience by catching errors early and providing better code structure. Additionally, I experienced the benefits of serverless architecture. It significantly streamlined the deployment process, thanks to built-in features like logging, authentication, and network management.
