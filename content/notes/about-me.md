# About me

Hi, I'm **Alysia Alfi Annora** (she/her), a front-end developer based in Yogyakarta, Indonesia, with 4+ years of experience building modern, interactive web interfaces.

## What I do

I work mostly with Vue.js and TailwindCSS, and I also build full-stack apps with React, TypeScript and Express. I care about pages that are fast and clear, so I spend a lot of time on SEO and Core Web Vitals.

- **DomaiNesia** (2021 – now): I revamped and built over 90% of the company's landing pages, including in-page purchasing flows and dynamic pricing. When the team moved from jQuery to Vue.js, I helped map and clean up the old code.
- **Lunash** (2024 – now, freelance): I built about 70% of the core features of a fintech dashboard that connects in-house and third-party APIs. I also automated deployment and helped migrate the infrastructure from Google Cloud to AWS.

## Mentoring and speaking

Since late 2022 I've mentored at Rakamin Academy. I was named Best Mentor in the Virtual Internship Program in 2023, and my group became the best-performing one in the cohort. I've spoken at PyCon APAC 2024 (SEO optimization in Python) and DevFest Yogyakarta 2023 (AI integration workshop), and I founded PyLadies Yogyakarta, a women-in-tech community.

## Studying

I'm finishing a B.Sc. in Computer Science (online) at Bina Nusantara University, expected in October 2026, while working full time. Going back to the basics has made me a better problem solver. My favorite subjects are data structures, human-computer interaction, software engineering and NLP.
