# God, I love being young

Everyone who knows me would probably say I'm extroverted and very active. Arguably yes, and I love being that kind of person! That's how I'm spending my youth, and I'm grateful for it :)

Being young is one of the greatest blessings a human can receive. Youth comes with so much energy, curiosity and free will for literally everything. I try my best to combine those three in a way my older self will cherish.

I looooove having fun outside. I guess it started in childhood, when I spent 80% of my time outdoors. My friends and I were really into traditional games like petak umpet (hide and seek), engklek (hopscotch) and ketek menek, all played in groups and a bit competitive. The only digital game we cared about was Minecraft, because we could play it together. We created servers, messed around, and built our own basecamp.

I'm not the smartest in the room, but I treat education with great respect. I'm a big fan of lovely teachers who understand their students well: the ones who push a student when they see potential, and nurture them carefully when they feel lost. Sadly, teachers are not well protected by the law here, which makes me, and everyone, sad. Big cheers to teachers out there! I'm always rooting for you!

In high school, I won second place in an English debate competition with two friends. I was involved in several important school events, and I landed my first job through a company hiring process at school, then moved 800 km away from home to the company's HQ. All when i was 19.

Two years later, I moved to another company, again hundreds of kilometers from home, because I like being a stranger in someone else's hometown LOL. Here, I feel like I'm at my peak of being young. I explore this town and the towns next to it, and I even flew abroad for the first time.

Working while doing my undergraduate degree sounds scary, but I slayed it: I finished several running and cycling races and hiked several mountains, the tallest at 3,100 m above sea level. And now working on my mooscles (muscle) by hitting the gym to support my endurance. Gosh, I love being young, and I can feel it just by writing this.
